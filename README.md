# LearnSpace

LearnSpace is a workspace for learning online without getting pulled into unrelated recommendations. Find a lesson, ask questions as you study, and come back to your notes and progress when you're ready for the next session.

[Start learning on LearnSpace](https://learn-space.app/)

## What you can do

- **Find lessons and papers.** Get video and research paper recommendations based on your interests, or search for a topic you want to explore.
- **Ask questions as you watch.** The AI tutor uses the lesson transcript to explain confusing ideas and point you to the moment in the video that covers them.
- **Follow a learning path.** Work through topics in order, from foundational concepts to more advanced lessons and research papers.
- **Make a study plan.** Set a goal and get a calendar of what to watch, read, and practice.
- **Keep notes in context.** Build a study guide alongside the lesson so you can revisit the ideas and questions that came up.
- **Practice what you've learned.** Check your understanding with quizzes, or work through a coding exercise with a tutor that offers hints as you go.

## Get started

1. [Create an account](https://learn-space.app/register) and choose a few topics you're interested in.
2. Pick a lesson from your feed or choose a learning path if you want a clear sequence.
3. Try asking the tutor about a confusing part, then take a quiz to see what stuck.
